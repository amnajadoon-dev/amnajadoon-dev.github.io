What I do

Websites

Built from scratch, or from your Figma or PDF design, in Elementor, Divi, Gutenberg or WPBakery. Every site is mobile optimised. WooCommerce stores are my strong point.

I also take on Shopify (Liquid), Wix (Velo) and Squarespace projects.

Hosting and migration

Domain and hosting setup, plus website backup and migration from any host or domain to another, with zero data loss.

A full backup and a staging clone exist before anything moves. Then I check permalinks, forms and email.

SEO and speed

Basic SEO setup, including meta tags, so your site is ready to be listed on Google. Speed fixes so it loads fast.

On optimization jobs, I've moved PageSpeed and GTmetrix scores from the 40s and 50s to 70-80+.
