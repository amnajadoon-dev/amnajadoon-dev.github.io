How it works

No call needed unless you want one.

  1. Send me the URL

    Tell me what's wrong, your hosting provider, and whether you have admin access.

  2. Get your numbers

    PageSpeed and GTmetrix scores, the three things costing you the most, and a fixed price.

What I take on

Speed

I start with a PageSpeed and GTmetrix baseline, work out whether the builder, images or caching is the cause, and hand you the before and after scores.

Usually with LiteSpeed Cache, Cloudflare or SiteGround Speed Optimizer.

Migrations and hosting

Host to host, domain to domain, staging to live. A full backup and a staging clone exist before anything moves, so a bad cutover can be undone.

Afterwards I check permalinks, forms and email. I also set up DNS, SSL and professional email, on cPanel, WHM or Plesk.

WooCommerce repairs and builds

Checkout errors, cart bugs, payment gateways failing on mobile, a theme fighting a plugin. I also build stores from nothing, with custom PHP and jQuery where a plugin would be overkill.
